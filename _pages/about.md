I build machine learning models for healthcare that can tell when they might be wrong. Most of my work is about uncertainty: getting a model to say how confident it is, and making sure that confidence is honest, so a clinician knows when to rely on a prediction and when to take a closer look.

My current project, funded by an [FWO](https://www.fwo.be/en/) postdoctoral fellowship, applies this to sleep apnea, a common disorder that often goes undiagnosed. I develop models that help diagnose it from sleep and physiological signals, and that flag the cases where they are unsure.

Before this, I worked on patient monitoring with radar sensors and on models small enough to run on the device itself. I received my PhD in Computer Science from Ghent University in 2023.
